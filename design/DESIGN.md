## Brand & Style
This design system serves an ultra-luxury architectural materials portal catering to principal architects, interior directors, and high-end developers. The aesthetic combines uncompromising architectural minimalism with editorial precision. 

The emotional signature is stoic, curated, and absolute: zero extraneous ornamentation, rigorous structural alignment, and pristine material honesty. Visual tension is generated purely through deliberate scale contrasts, sharp edges, and stark negative space rather than chromatic distraction.

## Colors
The palette is strictly achromatic. Every value is derived from pure spectral neutrality without warm, cool, blue, or olive undertones.

- **Canvas & High Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure untinted white providing absolute illumination and clarity.
- **Deep Obsidian (`#000000`):** Pure dense black for primary typographical marks, structural anchor elements, and maximum contrast accents.
- **Charcoal (`#404040`):** Secondary tone for structural labels, subordinate data, and subdued icons.
- **Muted Steel (`#737373`):** Neutral gray calibrated for secondary metadata, subtle disclaimers, and quiet captions.
- **Architectural Rules (`#E5E5E5` and `#F0F0F0`):** Micro-borders, column guides, and spatial dividers that structure without closing off spatial flow.

## Typography
Typography creates tension between two worlds: Bodoni Moda delivers high-fashion editorial rigor with dramatic vertical stroke contrasts, while Geist provides surgical, technical exactitude.

- **Headlines & Editorial Marks:** Bodoni Moda is strictly applied to material categories, large-format numbers, editorial quotes, and high-level headings. It should never be set in heavy weights; the luxury resides in the hairline strokes of the 400 and 500 weights.
- **System, Data & Functional Text:** Geist serves specs, architectural coefficients, dimensional matrices, and interface controls. Set with precise line heights to maintain a tight typographic baseline.
- **Labels & Micro-Indicators:** Always uppercase with wide tracking (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) to reference architectural blueprints and luxury specimen catalogs.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a strict 12-column architectural grid with generous exterior margins that function like an art gallery matting.

- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12 columns, 64px (`4rem`) outer margin, 32px (`2rem`) gutters. Content maxes out at 1600px with generous margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px):** 8 columns, 32px (`2rem`) outer margin, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) outer margin, 16px (`1rem`) gutters.

Vertical rhythm is expansive: sections are divided by large spans (`space-xl`), allowing materials to be inspected in isolated focus.

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with pure architectural minimalism, this design system does not use shadows, blurry drop-shadows, or artificial depth layers. Depth is strictly planar and flat, defined through hairline structural borders and pure tonal contrast.

- **Planar Division:** Elements occupy the same elevation plane, distinguished via hairline rules (`1px solid #E5E5E5`) reminiscent of drafted CAD floorplans.
- **Hover & Active Depth:** Interactivity is communicated through total inversion (white surface snapping directly to solid `#000000` fill) or border tone escalation (`#E5E5E5` transitioning to `#000000`), never through z-axis drops or glow states.
- **Modals & Overlays:** Sheets and dialogs sit directly above the canvas separated by a high-contrast `#000000` hairline border and a solid white backdrop, grounded by an unblurred, solid `#000000` scrim set to 60% opacity.

## Shapes
A strict `0` roundedness rule governs the system. Every button, input container, card, modal, and badge has razor-sharp, zero-radius corners (`0px`). 

This deliberate geometric rigidity mirrors cut marble slabs, structural steel I-beams, architectural glass, and monolithic formwork, rejecting the friendly curved vernacular of consumer mobile apps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#000000` background, pure `#FFFFFF` typography (`label-lg`), `0px` radius, padding `16px 32px`. Hover shifts immediately to an outline state: solid `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #000000` border, `#000000` typography.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1px solid #000000` border, `#000000` typography. Hover inverts to solid `#000000` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Text Action:** Unbordered, `#000000` uppercase label with an animated 1px underline extending from 0 to 100% width on hover.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Single bottom rule or 1px perimeter box using `#E5E5E5`. No fill (`transparent`).
- Focus transitions border instantly to `1px solid #000000`. Placeholder text sits in `#737373` (`body-md`), transitioning to `#000000` upon input. Error states are signaled through a bold `2px solid #000000` border and an uppercase micro-label, strictly avoiding colored alerts.

### Cards & Material Specimen Tiles
- Framed in `1px solid #E5E5E5` with `#FFFFFF` backgrounds.
- High-resolution material photography is presented edge-to-edge or seated with equal `space-md` internal padding.
- Card hover states trigger a border change to `#000000` or reveal technical metadata overlayed in solid `#000000` on `#FFFFFF`.

### Chips & Badges
- Sharp `0px` corner rectangles.
- Default state: `1px solid #E5E5E5`, `#404040` uppercase text (`label-sm`), padding `4px 10px`.
- Selected/Active state: Solid `#000000` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp squares (`0px` radius) for both types (radios use diamond or internal nested square motifs instead of circles to preserve geometric sharpness).
- Border: `1px solid #000000`. Checked state: solid `#000000` fill containing a pure white architectural crosshair or solid inner square block.

### Material Specification Tables
- Minimalist, high-density data tables with horizontal hairlines (`#E5E5E5`) and zero vertical borders.
- Column headers set in uppercase `label-md` (`#737373`). Numerical spec data (PSI, density, finish) set in `Geist` monoline format for alignment.
